# Terms of use

These are the default terms of a GameHub hub. Whoever runs a hub can replace them with their own
(`terms.md` in the hub's data directory); changing them asks every account to accept them again.

1. **Your content.** Games, libraries, interfaces and comments you publish stay yours. By
   publishing them on this hub you grant everyone the right to view, play, fork and modify them
   under the license you declare in `game.toml` (CC-BY-4.0 if you declare none). A published
   version is permanent: other games may depend on it.
2. **Rules are mechanics, rulebooks are text.** Do not upload rulebooks, art or trademarks you
   do not own. Write rules in your own words and use generic names where a game's name is a
   trademark.
3. **Fair play.** Do not use bots in matches marked "humans only", do not manipulate ratings
   (multiple accounts, arranged results) and respect the other players.
4. **Fair use of resources.** Jobs (analysis, arenas, balance reports), storage and requests have
   quotas. Do not try to get around them or to attack the hub or its users.
5. **Moderation.** Content can be reported. The hub's moderators may hide repositories and
   suspend accounts that break these terms or the law.
6. **No warranty.** The hub is provided as is, without warranties; its administrators are not
   liable for lost data or interruptions. Keep your own copies (it is all git).
7. **Privacy.** The hub stores your account name, the e-mail you give it, your published
   content, your matches and ratings. It does not sell them. Passwords are stored only as salted
   hashes.

# Condiciones de uso (traducción)

1. **Tu contenido** sigue siendo tuyo; al publicarlo permites verlo, jugarlo, bifurcarlo y
   modificarlo según la licencia de tu `game.toml` (CC-BY-4.0 si no declaras ninguna). Una versión
   publicada es permanente.
2. **Las reglas son mecánicas; los reglamentos, texto.** No subas reglamentos, arte ni marcas
   ajenos.
3. **Juego limpio:** nada de bots en partidas "solo humanos" ni de manipular las valoraciones.
4. **Uso razonable:** los trabajos, el almacenamiento y las peticiones tienen cuotas.
5. **Moderación:** se puede denunciar contenido; la moderación puede ocultar repositorios y
   suspender cuentas.
6. **Sin garantías:** el hub se ofrece tal cual; guarda tus copias (es git).
7. **Privacidad:** se guardan tu nombre de cuenta, el correo que des, lo que publicas, tus
   partidas y valoraciones; las contraseñas, solo como hash con sal.
